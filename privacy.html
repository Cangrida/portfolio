Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

What this is

"postbot" is a personal scheduling tool built and used by one person, Galina Cangrida, to publish her own artwork videos to her own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other people and has no other users.

What it accesses

The app asks for a single Google permission: uploading videos to the YouTube channel of its owner (youtube.upload). It cannot read, edit or delete anything else in the Google account.

What it stores

An access token is stored in a file on the owner's own computer, only to let the app upload videos without signing in again. No data from Google or YouTube is collected, copied to any server, sold, shared or used for advertising.

Contact and revoking access

Questions: cangridagalexin@gmail.com. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
